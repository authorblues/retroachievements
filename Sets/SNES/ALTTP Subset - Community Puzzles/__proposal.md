## Subset name: Community Puzzles

## Subset of [The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past](https://retroachievements.org/game/355)

## Which archetype does this subset proposal fall under?
Community Recognized Challenges

## What Subset Type would this fall under? (Bonus/Challenge/Exclusive/Specialty)
Bonus is my preference, but Challenge would be acceptable

## Why is this unfit for the base set?
Requires glitches, extremely challenging playthroughs and goals

## Why should this exist despite being unfit for the base set?
These are historically-significant puzzles built by members of the ALTTP speedrunning community primarily between the years of 2012-2016. They were designed to test each others' understanding and mastery of glitches that were fresh and new at the time, pushing the boundaries of the game. This is highly unique content that RA is uniquely positioned to enshrine in such a way that future generations can experience them anew.

## What is the estimated time to master this proposed subset and, if applicable, how difficult?
All of these tasks require significant domain-specific game knowledge and mastery of a variety of tricks and glitches. There are ~70 puzzles, each of which could take up to a few hours to solve and execute. I would expect mastery from a knowledgable ALTTP speedrunner to take at least 50 hours. Players with less experience, likely much more.
