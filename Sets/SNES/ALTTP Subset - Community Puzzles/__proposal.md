## Subset name: Community Puzzles

## Subset of [The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past](https://retroachievements.org/game/355)

## Which archetype does this subset proposal fall under?
Community Recognized Challenges. To be clear, these are not puzzles from the RA community, but rather, puzzles designed by ALTTP speedrunners that were used as prompts in races on the SpeedRunsLive website.

## What Subset Type would this fall under? (Bonus/Challenge/Exclusive/Specialty)
Exclusive

## Why is this unfit for the base set?
Requires glitches, extremely challenging playthroughs and short goals

## Why should this exist despite being unfit for the base set?
These are historically-significant puzzles built by members of the ALTTP speedrunning community primarily between the years of 2012-2016. They were designed to test each others' understanding and mastery of glitches that were fresh and new at the time, pushing the boundaries of the game. This is highly unique content that RA is uniquely positioned to enshrine and preserve in such a way that future generations can experience them anew.

## What is the estimated time to master this proposed subset and, if applicable, how difficult?
All of these tasks require significant domain-specific game knowledge and mastery of a variety of tricks and glitches. There are ~70 puzzles, each of which could take up to a few hours to solve and execute. I would expect mastery from a knowledgable ALTTP speedrunner to take 50-100 hours. Players with less experience, likely much more. I'll have a better idea as I play through each of these to understand them better.

## Can you explain your set plan in detail?
Each prompt is [mirrored on my github](https://github.com/authorblues/retroachievements/tree/main/Sets/SNES/ALTTP%20Subset%20-%20Community%20Puzzles/prompts). As many of these as I am able to support will be given its own achievement, measured from the start of a save file, requiring single session completion. Each will award anywhere from 25 to 100 points, depending on the difficulty, which I will attempt to assign based on the expertise of a few friends in the ALTTP speedrunning community, as well as my own experience as I learn the tech necessary to complete them myself. I anticipate that there might be a few of these for which RA logic might be insufficient to implement, but I will do everything I can to support all of them.
