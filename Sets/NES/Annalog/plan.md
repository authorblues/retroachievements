Progression
- POLYCLOPS: Carnivorous Overgrowth
  - Defeat Polyclops, the carniverous overgrowth, and activate Power Circuit 1
  - Defeat Polyclops without taking damage or any computer errors (5)
- OUTBREAK: Mutant Abomination
  - Defeat Outbreak, the mutant abomination, and activate Power Circuit 2
  - Defeat Outbreak without taking damage or any computer errors, and without allowing any breakballs to touch the floor (5)
- DOOMGAZE: Cryptic Fiend
  - Defeat Doomgaze, the cryptic fiend, and activate Power Circuit 3
  - Defeat Doomgaze without any computer errors, and with a flower in hand (10)
- PYROFUGE: Lavaborn Spider
  - Defeat Pyrofuge, the lavaborn spider, and activate Power Circuit 4 (10)
  - Defeat Pyrofuge without taking damage or any computer errors (5)
  - Defeat Pyrofuge using only the left computer [program=6] (3)
- SALTWYRM: Gargantuan Annelid
  - Defeat Saltwyrm, the gargantuan annelid, and activate Power Circuit 5
  - Defeat Saltwyrm without taking damage or any computer errors
  - Defeat Saltwyrm using no more than 18 lines of code across both machines [programs=7,8] (5)
- BOMBSHELL: Antigravity Bivalve
  - Defeat Bombshell, the antigravity bivalve, and activate Power Circuit 6
  - Defeat Bombshell without taking damage or any computer errors
  - Destroy the back wall
- ALDA EGO: Forgotten Phantom
  - Defeat Alda Ego, the forgotten phantom, and activate Power Circuit 7
  - Defeat Alda Ego without taking damage or any computer errors
- CHIEF GRONTA: Defiant Warlord
  - Give Chief Gronta the B Module and earn the bad ending
  - Defeat Chief Gronta and either destroy or reactivate the complex
